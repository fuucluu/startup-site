How the calculator works

Start with contribution, not revenue.

Every average sale contributes what remains after the product cost and estimated card fee. Your booth and other event costs are paid from that contribution—not from gross revenue.

  1. Fixed event cost = booth fee + other event costs.
  2. Contribution per sale = average sale − product cost − estimated card fee.
  3. Break-even sales = fixed event cost ÷ contribution per sale, rounded up.
  4. Expected profit = expected transactions × contribution per sale − fixed event cost.
  5. Effective hourly earnings = expected profit ÷ total event hours.

Use a conservative forecast.

Foot traffic, weather, event fit, product mix, and customer behavior can change the result. Test a lower expected-sales number before paying a non-refundable booth fee. This calculator is a planning estimate, not financial or tax advice.
