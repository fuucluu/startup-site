Frequently asked questions

Is this just an Etsy fee calculator?

No. Editable selling fees are one part of a workflow connecting batch costing, price scenarios, event forecasts, and actual results.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No account is required. The downloaded application runs locally in a current browser.

Where is my information stored?

In that browser’s local storage. Download regular JSON backups, especially before clearing browser data or changing devices.

Does it calculate taxes?

No. Fair Margin is not accounting or tax software.

Fair Margin is a planning tool. Results depend on the costs, fees, sales, and other information entered by the user. It does not guarantee profit or sales and is not financial or tax advice.
